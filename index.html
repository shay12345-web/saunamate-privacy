SaunaMate Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

SaunaMate is a mobile app that helps people discover saunas in Ireland, explore sauna locations, track wellness sessions and save their favourite places.

This policy explains how information may be used when you use SaunaMate.

1. Information used by SaunaMate

Location: If you grant location permission, SaunaMate can use your device's location to help you explore nearby saunas. Location permission is optional, and you can change it in your device settings.

Session history and favourites: SaunaMate saves information such as sauna sessions, timers and favourite locations on your device so that these features work when you return to the app.

Notifications: If you enable notifications, SaunaMate may send an alert when your session timer finishes. You can disable notifications in your device settings.

2. Advertising

SaunaMate uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to support banner advertising on the Explore screen. During development, the app displays Google test advertisements. Live advertising will only be enabled after the necessary privacy and consent controls have been configured.

When live advertising is enabled, Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device identifiers, IP addresses, advertising interactions and diagnostic information, subject to applicable law and your privacy choices.

For more information, see Google's advertising privacy information.

3. Advertising consent

Where required, SaunaMate will request your consent before using personal information for advertising purposes. You may choose not to consent to personalised advertising.

We will provide a way to review or change applicable advertising privacy choices before enabling live ads.

4. Third-party sauna websites

SaunaMate provides links to independent sauna operators and booking websites. If you open one of these links, the external website may collect information under its own privacy policy.

SaunaMate does not control the privacy practices of these independent websites.

5. Why information is used

Information is used to provide app features you request, such as location-based discovery, saved sessions and notifications, and to support advertising where permitted.

Depending on the activity, processing may rely on your consent, your request to use an app feature, or legitimate interests where permitted by applicable law.

6. Storage and retention

Session history and favourites are stored locally on your device. You can remove locally stored app information by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app, subject to your device's operating system.

Third-party services, including Google, may apply their own data retention policies to information they process.

7. Your privacy rights

If you are in Ireland, the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of personal data, object to certain processing, and withdraw consent.

You may also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority, including the Irish Data Protection Commission where applicable.

8. Children's privacy

SaunaMate is intended for a general audience and is not specifically designed for children. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as SaunaMate develops, including when advertising or other features change. The latest version will be available on this page.
